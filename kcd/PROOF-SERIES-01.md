## Claim scope to be frozen before each physical examination

Every new PH-series entry must identify the **protected interior**, its invariant envelope, the surrounding **candidate relation**, the carrier class, any burden and transfer domains, the physical consequence route under examination, independent termination and measurement authorities, negative controls, alternate routes, changed-condition changes, and an explicit falsifier. KCD cannot be examined by asking only whether an object moved.

Before a claim can be called an *observed result*, it requires a frozen and approved apparatus and test envelope, measured pre-state, independent calibrated post-state or non-effect witness, uncertainty budget, actual effect-path inventory, positive and negative controls, receipt, same-condition replay, changed-condition replay and a clear bound on untested routes.

**Never equate a controller-generated decision, a synthetic seam result, or an internally signed receipt with measured physical non-formation.**

## Series 01 sequence

### Stage 0 — Category and proof-subject freeze

**Public activity now:** record the correct KCD class and non-equivalence boundary. Publish named PH-01..PH-04 proof objects and falsifiers. Keep KCD-A1, V.A., and Program B individually identified. This stage describes the objects but **does not confer instrumented physical standing**.

### Stage 1 — Release and apparatus readiness

**Publication is conditional.** The custodian must approve the exact public description, verify no protected math, construction details or customer data escape, and require any applicable qualified hardware safety review. For KCD-A1, the original Phase 0B formalization explicitly leaves numerical freeze, component qualifications, energized calibration, hazard review and independent sign-off open. No live hardware activation is authorized by this public document.

### Stage 2 — Bounded permitted-effect witness

Examine one already-authorized, approved low-risk carrier with fixed conditions. An independent measurement chain observes the actual protected-interior invariants and the declared exterior effect. Capture negative controls capable of distinguishing the claimed effect from ordinary causes. The physical measurement is *not* inferred from a numerical simulation or field diagram.

### Stage 3 — Refusal and physical non-formation

Alter **one material supporting condition**. A claim of physical refusal requires independent evidence that the **actual declared protected physical effect** did not form despite the attempted movement. Distinguish this from a `REFUSE` message, loss of telemetry or merely a command never being sent. Examine disclosed alternate paths separately.

### Stage 4 — Narrow, redirect and transfer

Where a lawful alternative is explicitly part of the bounded test, record what narrowed or redirected, what actually changed, and whether any transferred burden closed its declared accounting with uncertainty. **A successful alternative must not be inferred from a stopped original route.** Preserve independent measurements and the original failed-standing receipt.

### Stage 5 — Changed-condition replay and termination

Repeat the frozen condition and compare actual measured outputs. Then introduce a declared change in witness validity, capacity, route, standing or external relation. Preserve both original and child evidence. Demonstrate loss of standing or termination where expected, with **measured physical effect** and non-effect, not only reported program state.

### Stage 6 — Independent reproduction and scoped promotion

A distinct qualified reviewer repeats the bounded procedure against the same fixed source identity, with independent instrumentation as appropriate. Publish a precise **supported / partially supported / not reproduced / falsified / pending** disposition, plus any defects and route limits. An independent witness is not conferred by rerunning the same source inside the owner's continuous deployment workflow.

## Non-inheritance rules

- V.A.'s existing engineering and public synthetic examiner results cannot be counted as a KCD physical observation.
- KCD-A1 is one electromagnetic witness route, not the total universal law or whole KCD category.
- A successful result for an electromagnetic field does not prove acoustic, vibration, thermal, composite or all possible routes.
- Program B aperture or energy-recovery work **does not inherit** Program A standing and is **not** part of this series.
- Proof at one carrier and exact frozen version does not confer proof across versions, conditions or undeclared hardware.
- Protected foundational mathematics, private URK mechanics, numerical construction, safety-critical actuator configurations, and private client/pilot data remain excluded.

## Publication packet

Publish only after approval of a bounded, non-enabling record including:

- KCD-PH identifier; corrected source document/version; claim and falsifier
- frozen apparatus ID and digest; permitted field class and declared conditions
- public-safe independent sensor identity, calibration and uncertainty references
- actual observation of the protected effect or measured non-formation, with negative control
- changed-condition and alternate-route observation, where claimed
- hash-identified receipt, same-condition and changed-condition replay
- independent reviewer/examiner identity and limits, **only if independently verified**
- any disclosed safety, measurement, calibration and route-coverage limitation

The public [metadata preflight](../examiner/physical_packet_preflight.py) only checks presence and publication declarations; it cannot authenticate physical results. [Machine-readable KCD proof register](KCD-PROOF-REGISTER.json) binds the current publication status of each object; promotion requires attached, independently reviewable evidence and custodian release authority.
