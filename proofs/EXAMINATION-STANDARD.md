# Public proof standard

**A result deserves precisely the standing carried by its frozen evidence — no more and no less.**

This is the reader-facing application of the public *Shift Proof Estate and Challenge Standard*, July 2026, and the *True-Zero Consequence Systems Science — Reviewed Public Record*, v1.2.3, July 18, 2026. See [source identities](SOURCE-ESTATE.md).

## The seven-part review

| Check | Required showing | Failure that matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01 · Claim** | Exact proposition and what it does **not** assert | An implied universal result from a bounded example |
| **02 · Carrier** | Original artifact, build/run identity, digest and owner | A description or screenshot substituted for a run |
| **03 · Conditions** | Candidate, authority, standing, evidence, capacity and observation scope | An undisclosed change to a load-bearing condition |
| **04 · Positive control** | A permitted effect is actually observed | A boundary that blocks everything and is called safe |
| **05 · Negative control** | Invalid movement produces no measured protected effect | REFUSE in a log while the effect occurs |
| **06 · Change and replay** | Stable-condition repeat; material-change result; prior record preserved | Old standing continues, or an earlier receipt is rewritten |
| **07 · Route and witness** | Declared reachable route inventory, measured side effects, examiner identity | Alternate effect route untested or a self-check presented as independent |

A mathematical derivation, an executable artifact, an instrumented physical result, pilot operation and independent reproduction are separate evidence classes. A proof document may specify a mechanism without disclosing protected construction, but **a specified test is not a completed execution**.

## Native V.A. and a small public fixture are different objects

- **Native system proof:** original V.A. identity, declared governing conditions, consequence-boundary observations, receipt/replay and route coverage.
- **Public synthetic fixture:** a separately written example of how to examine a claim; may be reproducible but proves only its own tiny scope.
- **Historical pilot:** exact operational scope and dated original records; do not equate a pilot with universal or production-wide closure.
- **Independent witness:** the examiner must be distinct from the author/design/test owner and preserve their own record. Inviting review is not the same as having received independent reproduction.

When exact native records or pilot packets are outside this GitHub release, label them **NOT ATTACHED HERE**, not *nonexistent*, *failed*, or *unverified in all contexts*. Only the public standing is bounded.

## A falsifiable consequence statement

Fix one candidate movement and one protected effect. Measure before/after effect state with a separate instrument where possible. Revoke one material standing condition and attempt the same candidate. Record both the decision and the actual effect/non-effect; try every declared reachable alternate route. Preserve both original and new receipts and perform a same-condition replay. A bypass, effect despite refusal, or materially divergent replay defeats the bounded claim.

## Evidence handling

Never post paid customer pilot data, private source, credentials, unapproved images/logs, or proprietary construction to the public repository. A customer-approved redacted packet can still preserve a fixed build/run digest, changed-condition record, consequence measurement, witness and disposition.
