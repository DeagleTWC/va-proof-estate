## What version continuity requires

A valid historical-to-current comparison preserves the original V1.2 records **as historical originals** and identifies V12 with its own frozen release identifier, source/proof carrier fingerprint, governing conditions and consequence observations. A link between the two requires an explicit provenance record of what changed and which invariants remained relevant; it cannot be inferred from a shared name.

A native V12 public proof packet should therefore contain, to the extent release rights permit:

1. Original V12 release/build ID and cryptographic digest;
2. Precisely bounded claim, candidate and governing conditions;
3. Before/after observation of the protected effect, including one permitted case and one invalid case;
4. Changed-condition test and the status of alternate effect routes;
5. Immutable original receipts, tamper behavior, and replay evidence;
6. Attribution of the operator, technical reviewer and independent witness **only if an independent review was actually completed**;
7. A public-safe release authorization covering any customer or partner evidence.

These are **required release fields**, not assertions that the V12 carriers are missing from the private corpus or that V12 did not execute successfully.

## Public review rule

**Current system V12 ≠ website v2.0 ≠ historical Deploy Build V1.2 ≠ scientific document v1.2.3.**

Do not write “V12 verified” on the strength of an older, distinct packet. Do not retroactively rename V1.2 proof carriers as V12. Do not portray a synthetic mock fixture as native computational-substrate evidence.

This registry clarifies public naming, not the protected implementation. **Evidence is open to examination; protected construction remains under custody.**
