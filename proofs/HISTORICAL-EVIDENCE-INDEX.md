## Carrier-level inventory: add originals without overwriting lineage

A public entry for each safely releasable historic artifact should preserve:

1. Original project/layer designation, exact build or freeze revision and document/object owner;
2. Freeze creation date and immutable digest, with the original file retained by its custodian;
3. Bounded claim, governing standing, attempted consequence and covered routes;
4. Pilot/run IDs, original result/effect or non-effect observations, negative controls and changed-condition attempts **when those exist in that original**;
5. Unmodified receipts and the original replay carrier;
6. Witness identity and method only where separately attributable original examiner evidence is held;
7. Any access, distribution, customer confidentiality or NDA restriction;
8. This site's **specific publication status**, which must not be confused with the historical object's existence or private examination status.

Start by cataloguing historical freeze IDs and dates **from the unchanged originals**; do not re-freeze an older original merely to pretend it was newly created. A newly public redacted extract can add review access without changing an earlier original or asserting that a past examiner repeated a new October test.

## Publication status definitions

- **HISTORICALLY RECORDED / ORIGINAL UNDER CUSTODY:** Original source or record is identified elsewhere; this public repository does not expose all its underlying evidence.
- **PUBLIC CARRIER LINKED:** Exact release-cleared bytes, hash and observation scope can be inspected by a public reviewer.
- **PUBLIC BOUNDED RESULT EXAMINED:** Original observation and its declared conditions can be checked from publicly available carriers.
- **PUBLIC INDEPENDENT REPRODUCTION:** Distinct examiner attribution, preserved original run, reproducible procedure and independence checks are attached.

These publication labels are **not a retrospective grade of the protected underlying system**. Keep the original native, synthetic and physical registers in their proper scopes.
