# Historical third-party examinations — original custody and qualified credit

**Record status:** A previously conducted bounded technical examination of an externally submitted frozen artifact is recorded under controlled source custody. **Its original submission and reviewer correspondence are not publicly attached here.** No outside reviewer reproduction of native V.A. is conferred by that examination.

The Proof Estate has two distinct, legitimate historical credit lanes:

1. **Review practice:** The examiner reviewed *someone else's* frozen engineering artifact, checked its original identity, executed or examined its bounded tests, found issues where evidence did not support claimed standing, and preserved the final limited disposition. This can support the examiner's technical examination history **only when the original review record may be released and verified**.
2. **Independent examination of V.A.:** A qualified outside person examines an actual frozen V.A. native carrier, runs controlled adversarial and changed-condition tests, observes the protected consequence independently, and preserves their own signed original report. **That is a different event.** It cannot be inferred from a review of third-party work, a signed receipt alone, or peer agreement.

[Machine-readable third-party publication register](THIRD-PARTY-REVIEW-REGISTER.json) begins with one anonymized, **unreleased** historical review pointer. It contains no private correspondence, original external package, results, partner identity, source hash, signing keys or claim of independent V.A. reproduction. A zero public-carrier count is not an assertion that no review happened historically.

## Release route

For any historical third-party result offered for credit, obtain written authorization to disclose the original, retain a frozen identity and exact SHA-256, record who built what, when and what the examiner actually ran, preserve the examiner's own challenge and original observations, record every falsification or unresolved route, and attach the disposition **including partial qualification or HOLD where applicable**.

A reviewer who tested somebody else's engineering may receive credit for that bounded technical review. The submitter may receive credit for their demonstrated behavior. **Neither receives credit for outcomes outside the fixed carrier.** Native V.A. and physical KCD examination remain separate.

## Bring your own workflow for the same treatment

The [BYO Workflow Evidence Route](../examiner/BYO-WORKFLOW-EVIDENCE.md) invites any authorized engineer or institution to submit their own workflow for local data-only evidence checks followed by independent testing and human qualification. **Evidence should be promoted by actual preserved third-party results and real independent observation — not by the number of testimonials or submitted PDFs.**
