# Milestone 11 — Historical Evidence Recovery and Source Reconciliation

**Status:** Source-family reconciliation delivered. Original private files remain under custody. This step does not invent new freezes, overwrite historical objects or conclude how many pilots previously ran.

The Proof Estate now distinguishes **five source families** located in historical material: an earlier frozen engineering proof package, bounded live-workflow scope and acceptance conditions, original launch/execution-lock packages, a source-stage first-run form, and review of a frozen *third-party* engineering artifact. These are **five classified evidence families**, **not five total pilots** and **not five independently reproduced native V.A. runs**.

## What was recovered and why it matters

Previous source review linked a historical engineering verifier's recorded eight-case proof-base checks to a **different** later controlled pilot workflow. A pilot launch packet and an execution form must be evaluated separately: evidence of a delivered package cannot substitute for actual positive/negative observed effects; a blank result form does not cancel past or later real tests from other original records. This distinction prevents both incorrect evidence promotion and unjustified dismissal of the broader historical estate.

Original ZIPs and customer files are **not** published here. Some originals are located in restricted Library/email attachment custody and have not been re-executed or independently hashed by this website. The published reference is an intentionally **non-sensitive family index**; exact original file locators, correspondence, payment details, people, original source hashes and unreleased review results remain outside the public page.

## How a new historic original enters the estate

1. Retrieve the actual unchanged original from authorized custody; record its original build/freeze ID, byte SHA-256, original date and custodian.
2. Preserve distinct evidence stages. A frozen build test is not an executed customer-pilot run; launch is not production acceptance; an outside engineering review is not an independent native V.A. reproduction.
3. Inspect the exact result and changed-condition, negative-control, bypass-route and replay carriers, **including failures**. Keep old unresolved dispositions, do not rewrite them.
4. Determine legal release authority for each original, customer workflow and third-party submission.
5. Create a separately hashed public-safe extract without altering the original, and pass the [historical admission gate](historical/ADMISSION-GUIDE.md) and qualified human review.
6. Update the relevant **specific** public claim only after independent inspection; do not promote the V12 or physical KCD evidence register based on another historical project.

Local tools: [offline original fingerprinting](historical/LOCAL-INVENTORY.md) and [multiple-pilot local evidence check](MULTI-PILOT-EVIDENCE-ROUTE.md). The new [source-family integrity validator](../scripts/validate_historical_source_families.py) protects the publication classification and non-inheritance boundary in CI.
