REVIEW METHOD AND EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
The review should remain inside the public record. It should neither infer undisclosed mathematics nor 
demand their publication.
1. Fix the reviewed object - Record repository, branch or release, commit identifier, environment, and 
review date.
2. Inspect the supplied artifact - Use the published instructions, code, tests, receipts, logs, or proof objects 
actually provided. Do not substitute a post or diagram for system output.
3. Separate claim from evidence - Record the claim, what was executable or inspectable, what output was 
produced, and what could not be independently confirmed.
4. Assess reproducibility only where supported - Repeat a public case when the repository supplies 
sufficient instructions, dependencies, and test material. Do not claim reproduction where it was not 
performed.
5. Examine changed-condition behavior only where supplied - Determine whether the disclosed changed 
condition alters the result while preserving the earlier record.
6. Test sufficiency for the bounded claim - Ask whether the artifact supports the specific public claim 
being made - not whether it reveals the protected substrate.
7. Preserve category accuracy - Do not reclassify The Shift as compliance, monitoring, audit, attestation, 
workflow governance, a policy overlay, or conventional runtime control.
A PRIMARY PUBLIC REVIEW QUESTION
Are the claimed outputs produced by the disclosed artifact, reproducible under its
published conditions where reproduction is supported, and sufficient for the bounded
public claim being made?
