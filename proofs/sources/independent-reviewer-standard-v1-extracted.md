1. Purpose and Scope
This standard defines the minimum evidence required for a person to claim qualification as an 
independent technical reviewer of high-consequence systems. It separates internal checking, self￾audit, peer review, independent reproduction, artifact inspection, and category review. It is designed 
to prevent self-validation from being presented as independent qualification.
This document does not certify any reviewer, endorse any system, disclose protected construction, or 
substitute for discipline-specific legal, regulatory, safety, security, or professional-engineering 
requirements.
Category boundary
A review method is not reviewer standing. A checklist is not independence. A self-review may 
demonstrate internal discipline, but it cannot establish independent review capability on its own.
2. The Independence Condition
Independent review requires separation between the origin of the work and the person accountable 
for checking it. The reviewer must not be the designer, architect, author, implementer, scope owner, 
test author, publisher, or prior advisor for the artifact under review, unless the review is explicitly 
classified as internal self-checking rather than independent review.

4. Admissible Qualification Evidence
A qualification submission must be based on a completed review of a genuinely third-party artifact. 
The reviewer must arrive with independence already established. The qualification process must not 
be used to manufacture the missing design check or to obtain the independent review the applicant 
should already have brought as evidence.
Third-party origin
The artifact was not designed, scoped, implemented, published, or previously advised on by the 
reviewer.
Fixed carrier
The exact artifact, repository state, commit, version, file, build, or released package is identified.

Claim separation
The author's stated claims are distinguished from what the artifact and evidence actually 
demonstrate.
Independent reproduction
The reviewer reproduces the available evidence without relying on the author's conclusion as proof.
Independent challenge
The reviewer introduces counterexamples, changed conditions, refusal paths, or alternate-route 
challenges not merely copied from the author's test suite.
Unknown preservation
Missing evidence, unresolved conditions, and inaccessible claims remain unknown rather than being
promoted to pass or fail.
Proportionate conclusion
The final classification does not exceed the fixed carrier, observed evidence, or declared conditions.
Public inspectability
A third party can inspect the review path, evidence map, limitations, and conclusion without 
protected-mechanism disclosure.
5. Review Formation Conditions
Before any technical procedure begins, the review itself must have standing. The following 
conditions are formation requirements, not optional workflow preferences:
1. Object exists: The submission is sufficiently complete and fixed to be reviewed. An unfinished 
design cannot be presented as completed qualification evidence.
2. Design accountability is identified: The designer/architect and the independent checker are 
named or explicitly marked as absent.
3. Reviewer independence exists: The reviewer has no authorship, design, implementation, 
scoping, publication, or advisory ownership that defeats independence.
4. Claim boundary exists: The exact claims and non-claims are fixed before findings are issued.
5. Evidence carrier exists: The review points to inspectable implementation, test, receipt, run, 
calculation, drawing, record, or other carrier.
6. Authority and limitation are declared: The reviewer states what the review can and cannot 
determine.
Formation refusal
If the artifact is unfinished, the review is premature. If the artifact is presented as finished but has 
only been checked by its own designer, independent verification is absent. The qualification object

8. Public-Safe Category Alignment
This standard aligns with the public-safe category rule that vocabulary, status, branding, claims, and 
diagrams do not establish category standing. The carrier must hold. The Shift Public Category Packet 
distinguishes downstream governance, audit, permission, execution control, and evidence surfaces 
from consequence-boundary proof, and states that the seam decides rather than vocabulary.
For reviewer qualification, the equivalent rule is: the title “reviewer” does not establish reviewer 
standing. Independence, the fixed carrier, the evidence relation, and the bounded conclusion must 
hold.
Public rule
No independent relation, no independent review. No fixed carrier, no defensible finding. No 
evidence separation, no qualification.

9. Disqualifying or Corrective Conditions
• Self-review presented as independent qualification.
• The reviewer and designer/checker fields identify the same accountable person.
• An unfinished design is submitted as completed reviewer evidence.
• The qualification authority is asked to perform the missing independent check.
• The reviewer relies only on the author's test suite and calls it independent reproduction.
• Missing evidence is promoted to PASS rather than preserved as unknown or HOLD.
• Vocabulary similarity is treated as category or architectural equivalence.
• A narrow result is promoted into complete-system, certification, endorsement, or category standing.
• Protected mechanics are requested before the public-safe carrier is shown.
10. Reviewer Declaration
By signing this record, the reviewer declares that the reviewed artifact is not their own work; that 
they did not design, scope, implement, publish, or advise on the artifact in a manner that defeats 
independence; that all findings are bounded to the fixed carrier and observed evidence; and that 
unknowns, limitations, and non-claims have been preserved.
