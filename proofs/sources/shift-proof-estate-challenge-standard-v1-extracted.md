04 Public Review Method
1
Fix the reviewed object
Record repository, branch or release, commit identifier, environment, and review date.
2
Inspect the supplied artifact
Use the published instructions, tests, code, receipts, logs, and proof objects actually provided. Do
not substitute a post or diagram for system output.
3
Separate claim from evidence
Record what is claimed, what was executable or inspectable, what output was produced, and what
could not be independently confirmed.
4
Test repeatability only where supported
Do not claim reproduction where instructions, dependencies, or test material were insufficient.
5
Examine changed-condition behavior only where supplied
Determine whether the changed condition alters the result while preserving the earlier record.
6
Test sufficiency for the bounded claim
The question is not whether protected internals were disclosed. The question is whether the public
object supports the specific public claim.
7
Preserve category accuracy
Do not reduce the system to compliance, monitoring, audit, attestation, workflow governance,
policy overlay, or conventional runtime control.

05 Bounded Technical Challenge Standard
A serious challenge identifies a precise public object, condition, expected result, observed result,
and evidence sufficient for independent review.
A VALID CHALLENGE MAY SHOW
+ A disclosed output is not produced under its published conditions.
+ Same-condition replay does not reproduce the bounded result where replay is supported.
+ A supplied changed condition fails to alter continuation as claimed.
+ An earlier receipt or record can be rewritten without detection.
+ A required refusal does not fail closed.
+ Unsupported movement reaches the protected effect through a route the public claim says is
closed.
+ The artifact cannot support the category claim attributed to it.
THE FOLLOWING DO NOT MEET THE STANDARD
+ General disagreement with terminology.
+ Similarity of vocabulary, diagrams, gates, receipts, refusal language, or governance claims.
+ Demanding publication of protected mathematics as the price of reviewing a bounded public
claim.
+ Treating the absence of protected material from a public packet as proof that it does not exist.
+ Reviewing one projection as though it were the complete dimensional system.
CHALLENGE
FORMULA
Object + condition + expected output + observed output + evidence + replay
state.
Anything less is commentary, not a bounded technical challenge.
