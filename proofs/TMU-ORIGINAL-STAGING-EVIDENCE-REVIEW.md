## Why the raw originals are not public yet

Release of the actual original receipt values, filenames that identify operational internals, raw source logs or partner-specific payloads is **not automatically permitted** merely because they were available to a collaborator. Under the charter, written exact-source authority, deidentification, risk assessment and human source/reviewer checks must precede disclosure.

The newly authored [public machine-readable inventory](TMU-RECORDED-STAGING-CANDIDATE.json) contains a bounded source-stage summary only; it does **not** attach the seven raw originals or claim an already release-cleared pilot carrier. A private original-byte audit and separately sealed email-source custody copy are retained for authorized examination.

An offline [seven-file source reconciliation checker](../scripts/verify_tmu_staging_originals.py) accepts private originals and an external digest manifest, validates file hashes and case correspondence **without executing the runtime**. Its synthetic tests are not additional historical runs. The broader [TMU candidacy record](TMU-PUBLIC-EVIDENCE-STATUS.json) still treats separately controlled real TMU partner/customer execution as **not established** by draft matrices or these staging outputs.
