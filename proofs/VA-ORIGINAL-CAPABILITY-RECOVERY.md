## The measurable upgrade bar

A source title, demo, marketing grade or its test count does not prove that its function exists in native V.A. Every lane must demonstrate the **ten independent quality criteria** listed in [the machine-readable source register](VA-ORIGINAL-CAPABILITY-RECOVERY.json): original source and lineage; live bind authority; frozen candidate and route; integrity and custody; measured positive and negative consequences; material change; alternate-route accounting; genuine receipts/replay; qualified independent human examination and exact-byte disclosure rights.

**“Ten times better” is a requested engineering target, not an achieved performance measurement.** No dimension-specific factor is established until we freeze an actual comparative baseline, define like-for-like metrics and conditions, and retain reproducible real tests showing a ratio of at least 10 where meaningful. Coverage ratios do not establish substrate superiority, native real-world consequence, safety certification, or throughput gains.

The [read-only registry integrity gate](../scripts/verify_va_original_capability_recovery.mjs) rejects dropped source references, accidental third-party assimilation, incorrect mapping, self-awarded V.A. native effects, rights flags, M28 promotion, or fabricated 10x standing. Its [synthetic adversarial checks](../scripts/test_va_original_capability_recovery.mjs) confirm that these status errors cannot silently enter the index. This is **public catalog integrity**, not V.A. execution.
