## Reader route

1. **Identity** — Freeze the original native build/release identity and SHA-256 digest. Preserve its source and custodian rather than relabeling an older or downstream demonstration.
2. **Claim** — Name one consequence and its governing conditions. Specify the limits of the test.
3. **Permitted observation** — Attach an original bounded positive-control result with an observed protected effect.
4. **Refused observation** — Attach an original bounded negative-control result with measured absence of protected effect. A refusal message is not a measurement.
5. **Changed condition** — Preserve the original prior standing and show exactly what material change altered the consequence decision.
6. **Routes and replay** — Declare examined alternate effect routes, acknowledge untested routes, and preserve unmodified original receipts and same-condition replay.
7. **Independent examination** — Attach independently produced records from distinct qualified witnesses **only after** the witnessed tests actually occur.
8. **Release authority** — Verify ownership, customer confidentiality, collaborator rights, protected mathematics, sensitive infrastructure and publication scope before uploading.

An original source may support a smaller claim than the system as a whole. That smaller claim remains valuable when bounded precisely.
