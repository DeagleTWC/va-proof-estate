## Bring Your Own Workflow — direct examination of actual work

The [BYO workflow standard](../examiner/BYO-WORKFLOW-EVIDENCE.md) is not merely a marketing intake. It supplies a **local evidence-building and qualification route**:

1. Define the precise original workflow, source/custody and protected consequence the applicant is authorized to test.
2. Preserve frozen original identity and validate archive coverage before making any behavioral statement. An earlier failed seal must remain historically failed; a separately reissued source gets a distinct identity.
3. Execute only the permitted bounded corridor; retain effect/expected non-effect and every material alternative route, not only the decision text.
4. Change one governing material condition, repeat/refuse as appropriate, preserve original receipts, observer measurements and replay with known failure controls.
5. Independently verify authentic reviewers and raw external measurements; a signed JSON and a different key alone do not establish human independence.
6. Produce a minimal specifically authorized public extract with a **new hash** and exact original reference. Keep source, credentials and customer identity private unless lawfully cleared.

The [public issue-based examination request](https://github.com/DeagleTWC/va-proof-estate/issues/new?template=independent-effect-review.yml) accepts only non-confidential details. Do not upload private originals or sensitive payloads in a public issue.
