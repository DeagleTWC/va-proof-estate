# Claim-to-Evidence Dependency Map

This map states **necessary evidence relationships**, not a simulation or an operational proof.

```text
IDENTIFIED CLAIM + EXACT OBJECT
            |
            v
IMMUTABLY IDENTIFIED ORIGINAL CARRIER
            |
            v
FROZEN BUILD / CONDITION / SCOPE / SOURCE PROVENANCE
            |
            v
OBSERVATION OF THE DECLARED RESULT OR NON-RESULT
            |
            v
CHANGED MATERIAL CONDITION + NEGATIVE CONTROL
            |
            v
ALL REACHABLE ALTERNATE ROUTES CHECKED OR EXPLICITLY EXCLUDED
            |
            v
ORIGINAL RECEIPTS + INTEGRITY + SAME-CONDITION REPLAY
            |
            v
SEPARATELY ATTRIBUTABLE INDEPENDENT REPRODUCTION
            |
            v
HUMAN TECHNICAL DISPOSITION AT A BOUNDED CLAIM LEVEL
```

## Track separation

| Track | What may be established | What cannot be inherited |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formal source | Source identity, mathematical/formal statements and declared proof obligations | No observed native or physical effect |
| Synthetic adapter | Local behavior of the specified public interface under declared test cases | No native V.A. construction, external consequence, independent witness or KCD physical outcome |
| Native V.A. | Only effects supported by frozen native original carriers and measured/examined consequences | A passing mock, a signed statement, a prose description or an unrelated build named v12 |
| Physical KCD | Only a bounded phenomenon supported by separately calibrated instrument readings, changed-condition/negative trials and reproduction | No automatic Program A-to-B inheritance, or non-formation inferred from a drawing or metadata preflight |

## Failure routing

- **Missing carrier:** mark the claim as declared or not attached to this publication; never synthesize a substitute.
- **Broken checksum, provenance or chronology:** stop admission and retain the defect.
- **Unobserved physical effect, weak measurement coverage or unknown bypass:** do not promote to observed consequence status.
- **Single operator or keys with unverified owners:** remain a local or signed-data result, not independent reproduction.
- **Failed challenge or contradictory reports:** retain original failures and differences for examination, do not silently overwrite.

An examiner can use the [measurement coverage worksheet](MEASUREMENT-COVERAGE-WORKSHEET.md) to record which dependency is actually instrumented. The [public claim register](CLAIM-TO-CARRIER.json), [native V12 status](V12-EVIDENCE-STATUS.json) and [KCD register](../kcd/KCD-PROOF-REGISTER.json) are distinct authoritative public status carriers.
