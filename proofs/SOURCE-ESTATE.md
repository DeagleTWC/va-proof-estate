## What the originals substantiate as a source record

**The Shift — Public Proof Estate and Challenge Standard**, §§3–5, describes an inspection route spanning early bounded demonstrations, pre-effect refusal, authority revocation, scope expiry, revalidation, and live-state change. Its rule explicitly requires that a repository description not substitute for inspection of its output.

**True-Zero Consequence Systems Science**, §§7–12, separately names formalized records, corpus-recorded results, demonstrated behavior, replay-supported behavior, public inspection, independent reproduction, pilot and production. Its build/pilot section records a bounded Veritas Aegis intake-to-commit-or-block corridor and other defined build cases; it does **not** place each original run packet in this website.

**Independent Reviewer Qualification Standard**, §§1–4, separates internal checking from independent checking and sets a qualification burden for outside examiners. A self-run is not independent reproduction.

These are source-backed statements about **what the documents record**. They do not create new measured operational results.
