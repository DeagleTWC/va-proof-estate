# Teacher Humor and Warmth — bounded design

## Intent

Terry wants the public Teacher to be approachable, patient and occasionally playful, taking inspiration from his good-natured, slightly mischievous humor without impersonating him. The visitor's question and evidence must remain primary.

## Approaches

- **Selected:** small situational warmth behavior within the existing Open Conversation Kernel. Explicit invitations to joke and standalone self-confusion can receive a gentle line; otherwise the Teacher keeps teaching in ordinary language.
- **Constant joke insertion:** rejected because it distracts from evidence and could trivialize pain or uncertainty.
- **New autonomous humor generation system:** rejected because the existing Teacher is a static, bounded public guide, not a general language model.

## Behavior and boundaries

- Humor is with the visitor, never at the visitor's expense; no mockery, identities, or shame.
- A short standalone "I'm confused" after a lesson offers patient clarification with a small harmless smile, keeping the topic and public sources in place.
- An explicit "tell me a joke" produces a gentle, clearly non-evidentiary joke and an invitation back to learning.
- A serious or protected request takes precedence. Do not add levity to medical distress, injury, safety, bereavement, private access, or coercion; do not treat "lol" inside a substantive inquiry as permission to change the answer.
- No new network access, persistence, private mathematics, gateway, or claim of self-training.
- Frankie and Teacher remain separate. The Teacher does not speak as Terry.

## Acceptance

- With and without a previous lesson, explicit gentle-humor invitations have appropriate UI results.
- After confusion during a lesson, the original topic remains available for a next "Why?" or evidence request.
- Protected material cannot be reached through a humor utterance.
- Serious questions containing humor words are routed normally; no joke overrides them.
- Tests and the public release audit pass.
