# Meaning-Preserving Teaching Kernel (MPTK) — approved short design

## Intent

The public Teacher currently answers even simple questions with a long, terminology-heavy paragraph. Terry approved one shared communication kernel serving the existing eleven public subjects: answer in ordinary language, relate patiently to the learner, preserve the original meaning and evidence boundary, and offer detail on request.

## Approaches considered

- Replace each topic's source text with simplified copy: rejected because it would remove the original bounded account and make source-specific replies harder to preserve.
- Generate a simplification from arbitrary user text: rejected for the static public Teacher because it could invent claims and contradict the finite evidence ceiling.
- **Chosen:** deterministic, reviewed plain-language lead-ins keyed to the established lesson, with the full original explanation available by disclosure. No new subject, remote call, inference engine, or protected source.

## Behavioral contract

1. For standard lessons, MPTK returns a concise everyday-language introduction (one to three sentences), including essential attribution and evidence limitations. The detailed original lesson is preserved, directly accessible and never rewritten in place.
2. Preserve the visitor's intended topic and field connection, the eleven teaching exercises, evidence class, source links, and private-access routing.
3. For a context-specific public response whose summary differs from the subject summary (e.g., particular mathematics status, pilot record, a Basin water/horizon question, or a repair conversation), do not silently replace its content with a generic simplification. In that case the original response is primary.
4. No protected requests receive a public explanation; no new content transmission or storage.
5. Accessibility: native disclosure control, keyboard-operable; detailed content available when JavaScript runs and an ordinary no-script page still has its existing fallback messaging.

## Boundaries and evidence

- The existing eleven subject kernels remain unchanged. MPTK is a cross-cutting presentation kernel.
- Statements are explicitly attributed to the V.A./Natural Order public account as needed, not upgraded to physical evidence or universal verification.
- No private source files, architecture, math, Frankie memory, or unpublished KCD carriers are included.

## Success criteria

- A novice's “What is the Natural Order?” yields a short first-screen answer with a visible optional detailed explanation.
- All eleven subjects have an appropriate plain summary; user-specific source-bound answers keep their exact context.
- Current evidence links and legal access corridor remain intact.
- Tests, release audit and public Pages checks pass after publication.
