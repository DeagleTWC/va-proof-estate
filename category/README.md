## The category boundary in one statement

**Conventional systems often start with a formed action and ask whether to permit, inspect, record or reverse it. True-Zero systems begin earlier: establish the lawful possibility environment, resolve what can acquire standing, and prevent unsupported movement from acquiring protected consequence.**

A denial decision is not automatically non-formation. A signed receipt is not automatically custody. An authorized operator is not automatically entitled to continue under changed conditions.

## What "kernelizing the environment" means

The architecture does not begin by assuming an action is already executable and then asking a permission service to veto it. It establishes the governed conditions in which a candidate movement can acquire standing to form, continue, narrow, redirect, transfer, pause, refuse or halt. The proof obligation is not a reassuring output label: it is evidence of what actually formed or could not form along the declared protected consequence-bearing route.

For the **physical KCD** application, the inversion is explicit:

> The object is not forced through the environment. The container holds the protected interior. The kernel resolves which surrounding relations may lawfully continue.

A candidate movement may concern a field, heat, vibration, acoustic transmission, a surrounding boundary, burden routing, or a container/payload state. Payload displacement can be *one* witness; it is not the defining discovery.

## Public evidence bar

1. Identify the exact **claim, category, carrier and frozen version**.
2. Establish **present standing and governing conditions**, including protected interior, source validity, capacity and burden, geometry, uncertainty and alternative paths as applicable.
3. Show **an actual effect or its independently measured non-formation**, not merely the reported decision.
4. Change a material condition; preserve earlier evidence, attempt relevant alternate routes and capture the resulting state.
5. Provide **receipts, replay, reproducibility procedure and independent examiner status**.
6. Restrict conclusions to that exact scope; disclose limitations and falsifiers.

Evidence tiers must not merge: **FORMALIZED** is not **MEASURED**; **MEASURED** is not **INDEPENDENTLY REPRODUCED**.
