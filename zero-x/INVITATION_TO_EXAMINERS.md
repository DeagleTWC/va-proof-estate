# A small examination we can share

**An invitation, not a request for endorsement.**

Maria Kollia asked an important question: what would demonstrate that an unauthorized consequence was prevented, rather than merely receiving a refusal message?

V.A. ZERO-X offers a **bounded, synthetic example** that anyone can examine independently. It was prepared in response to that conversation and acknowledges CEOS and ZERO as separate work. Neither Maria nor her frameworks authored or endorsed this examination.

## Try it in two minutes

1. [Open the interactive case](https://deagletwc.github.io/va-proof-estate/zero-x/#maria-challenge) and select **Try the bounded example**.
2. Look at the 21 events: which movements are allowed, which are blocked, and where all 120 abstract workload units go.
3. Select **See why a refusal alone is not enough**. The examiner introduces an intentionally unsafe mock effect after refusal and reports the finding.
4. If helpful, change a condition, add an event, or describe your own bounded workflow. To compare *your* system's responses, download the JSON response template, run the case in an environment you control, and import non-sensitive responses. This page does **not** execute or connect to your implementation.

## What the case asks

Six hypothetical facilities, eight modeled consequence routes and changing authority, water, capacity, jurisdiction, evidence and timing conditions. The examination checks mock before/after effects, conserves the workload obligation, and rejects the claim that a refusal word by itself proves non-formation.

## What the case does not ask

No source disclosure, migration, license, account, approval, endorsement, or transfer of intellectual property. Visitors own their own workflows and retain their own evidence. No live facility, native V.A. kernel, or independent physical consequence is operated here. The 10–100-year menu describes input specifications, **not** a completed long-horizon native experiment.
