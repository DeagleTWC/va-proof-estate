1. Exact native system and version/build identity; original byte digest of the approved native binary/construct; run ID and trusted frozen configuration.
2. Original candidate commitments and the five governing invariant families for every test interval, including independent water/capacity evidence, authority, contradiction status and timebase.

4. Separately captured *actual consequence* state before/after each attempted binding, with observation instrument and examiner identity; a printed REFUSE is insufficient.
5. Cumulative obligation ledger: all 120 workload units and every site's water and capacity burden retained through split/repair/replay; no silent burden extinction.
6. Eight-corridor inventory and explicit route coverage. Unlisted routes remain outside the claim.
7. Original contemporaneous receipts with parent-child integrity, exact version/condition linkage, same-condition replay, and changed-condition re-resolution.
8. A separately attributable reviewer packet if independence is asserted; do not call an internally generated hash an independent witness.
9. Public-release authority for each exact evidence byte. Keep the protected mathematical operators, private keys, partner site details and unapproved originals out of the public repository.

## Qualification rule

`verify_packet.py` supports only a structural dry audit of an illustrative example. It deliberately cannot elevate self-authored outputs to native evidence. It fails closed unless explicitly told to check *synthetic structure only*. A separately authorized inspection of native originals and observers is still essential before anyone uses the word “verified native result.”
