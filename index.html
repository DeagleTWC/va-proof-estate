05 / Reviewer challenge standard

Challenge the
exact claim.

A challenge is not a comparison of slogans. It fixes an object, a declared condition, a prediction, an observation, evidence, and replay state.

01 / Fix the carrier. Identify the precise disclosed artifact and its status.

02 / Name the breach. Identify the required result and what the artifact actually did.

03 / Preserve the result. Hold original observations and replay separate from interpretation.

04 / Bound the conclusion. Neither a successful case nor a failed case automatically generalizes to the entire protected system.

Export produces a local JSON worksheet. It does not send your challenge to V.A.
