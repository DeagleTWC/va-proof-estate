04 / Examination rule

What counts as mathematical evidence?

Identify the exact theorem and hypotheses, source edition, derivation, numerical scope, reproducibility protocol and independent-review record. A matching file hash establishes byte identity, not the theorem's correctness. Formalized, observed, and independently reproduced outcomes must not be conflated.
