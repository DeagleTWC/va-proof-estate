## 1. What is proved for every smooth projective variety

Let `X` be any smooth connected complex projective variety of complex
dimension `n`, and let `0 <= p <= n`. Write

`B_p(X) = H^(2p)(X,Z)_free intersect H^(p,p)(X)` for the integral Hodge
class lattice (identified in rational cohomology), and
`A_p(X)` for the subgroup generated by the cohomology classes of **actual
codimension-p algebraic cycles**.

The rational Hodge Conjecture in `(X,p)` is equivalent to

`Sat_(B_p)(A_p) = B_p`, or equivalently `rank_Z(A_p)=rank_Z(B_p)`.

This is the *saturation equivalence* already formalized in child v9:
clearing denominators shows that a rational combination of cycle classes
gives a finite integral multiple, and the reverse inclusion is immediate.
It is **not** an automatic way to find the cycles or the complete Hodge basis.

### Theorem 1 — Unconditional classical degree closure

For **every** such `X`, the rational Hodge Conjecture holds in codimensions

`p in {0, 1, n-1, n}` (with duplicates removed).

Thus **every codimension for every smooth complex projective variety
of dimension at most 3 is covered**. This is a classical theorem, not a
new discovery and not the result of finite testing.

**Proof.** In codimension 0, the fundamental class `[X]` generates the
degree-zero Hodge space; in codimension `n`, the class of a closed point
generates top-degree rational cohomology. In codimension 1, the Lefschetz
`(1,1)` theorem says every integral `(1,1)` class comes from the first
Chern class of a line bundle and hence a divisor. For `n>=2`, choose an
ample hyperplane class `h=c1(O_X(1))`. The hard Lefschetz theorem gives
an isomorphism of rational Hodge structures, with type shift,

`L^(n-2): H^2(X,Q) -> H^(2n-2)(X,Q)`, `a -> h^(n-2) cup a`.

Given any rational `(n-1,n-1)` Hodge class `beta`, its unique preimage
`alpha` is a rational `(1,1)` class. The Lefschetz `(1,1)` theorem makes
`alpha` a rational divisor class. Therefore `beta = h^(n-2) cup alpha`
is represented by an algebraic intersection of this divisor with
`n-2` hyperplanes. Every remaining codimension when `n<=3` is one of
the four covered values. QED.

**Integral precision:** Hard Lefschetz is a rational isomorphism here;
it need **not** be a unimodular isomorphism of the integral lattices.
Consequently, a non-unit *integral* index does **not** refute the
*rational* Hodge statement. This directly explains the previous v10
edge-measured index-36 versus full-geometric index-1 distinction.

### Corollary — The first generally unresolved degree

After excluding these *unconditionally established degree ranges*, the
first possible open general pair is

`(dimension n, codimension p) = (4,2)`.

The remaining **general-theorem frontier** is

`n>=4` and `2<=p<=n-2`.

This does **not** mean every case in the frontier is open: particular
fourfolds (including the smooth split quadric Q4 examined earlier),
other special geometries, and certain higher-dimensional families are
already known. It means this formal record supplies **no theorem for
all smooth projective X** in those general pairs.

## 2. Integer-only witness transport (new executable measurement)

For the known one-cycle degree `p=n-1`, let `D_1,...,D_r` be declared
actual divisors whose classes form a complete rational Hodge basis in
degree 2, and let `b_1,...,b_r` be a declared integral Hodge basis in
degree `2n-2`. Supply the **integer** matrix `L` whose columns are the
classes of `D_i * h^(n-2)` in the target basis. If the supplied geometric
bases are complete and the matrix is authentic, Hard Lefschetz implies
`det(L) != 0`.

For an integral target coordinate vector `y`, set

`m = abs(det(L)) > 0`, `c = sign(det(L))*adj(L)*y`.

The adjugate identity gives the exact *integer* equality

`L*c = m*y`.

Therefore the explicitly algebraic cycle

`Z = sum_i c_i * (D_i intersect h^(n-2))`

has class `m*beta`, and `beta = [Z]/m` is rationally algebraic.
**This is exact denominator clearing, not empirical coefficient fitting.**

## 3. What is still required to close unrestricted Hodge

For **arbitrary** smooth complex projective `X` in every remaining
`n>=4, 2<=p<=n-2` case, we would need a proved, general mechanism that:

1. derives the **complete** rational `(p,p)` Hodge-class space from the
   real geometry, not a declaration, proxy, or finite Fourier observation;
2. produces **actual algebraic cycles** on that same `X` with classes
   spanning that space over `Q`;
3. proves source-to-geometry compatibility and the spanning assertion
   uniformly for all such `X`, with no skipped case.

The existence of an exact lattice meter (v10) or the integer transport
identity (v11) does **not** itself supply those middle-dimensional cycles.
This gap is mathematical, not a missing run parameter or a failed unit test.
