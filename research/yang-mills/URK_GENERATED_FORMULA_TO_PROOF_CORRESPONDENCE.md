## Formal structure

Let `D` be a declared mathematical domain, `I` its invariant set, and `C` the conditions that make an evaluation admissible.

- `G(D,I,C) = K_D`: the generator instantiates the domain mathematical kernel/formula.
- `K_D(x) = (r,w)`: the formula resolves the typed data `x`, yielding result `r` and witness `w`.
- `V_D(K_D,x,r,w)`: a proof verifier confirms the witness and its hypotheses.
- Only when a mathematical derivation establishes `V_D ⇒ P_D` **at the theorem's required scope** does the witness support the full proposition `P_D`.

This is **abstract notation for the intended method**, not the protected original generator's literal API or a theorem that an unspecified kernel proves Yang–Mills. Truth, conservation/no-loss, capacity, lawfulness and harmonic coherence are original URK source concepts. A release can protect formula internals while disclosing the mathematical statement, its domain, hashes, witness surface and verification reasoning.
