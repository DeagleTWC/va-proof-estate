02 / What makes the complete system different

Admission is not logging.
Refusal is not an icon.

A system can announce “denied” and still produce an effect. The examination standard therefore requires the exact protected consequence, boundary enforcement record and independently observed result. Here are the distinctions readers should carry through the entire estate.

Authority and admissibility

Identity, scope, active standing, evidence sufficiency and changed governing conditions are evaluated against the precise claimed action.

WHO HAS STANDING?
B / AT THE BOUNDARY

Consequence custody

DID EFFECT FORM?
C / AFTER DECISION

Original receipt and replay

Preserve original identity, recorded outcome, effect observation, chain, integrity and material-condition changes without rewriting history.

CAN IT BE RECHECKED?
D / INDEPENDENT REVIEW

Qualification and lawful continuation

Separate reviewers measure the consequence using actual instrumentation, retain failures and routes, and qualify only the proven scope.
