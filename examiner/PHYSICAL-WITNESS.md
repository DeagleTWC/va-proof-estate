# Physical Witness — Non-Enabling Examination Route

This page identifies **public-safe physical proof obligations**, not physical mechanism construction, operating instructions, or actual laboratory results. Original controlled mechanical/electrical apparatus records remain under custody and are **not attached by this publication**.

The mechanism classes in the controlled record include protected-interior / surrounding-relation examination; measurable intervention before physical consequence; loss-of-standing termination; and changed-condition witness/replay. A single electromagnetic bench embodiment must not be mistaken for the full family of physical mechanisms.

## Priority independent examination targets

1. **Protected-interior preservation:** freeze a physical test envelope and define what is protected; separately observe that the interior remains inside its declared invariant limits.
2. **Physical refusal / non-formation:** provoke a declared failure condition; establish by **independent measurement of the actual physical effect** that unauthorized actuation or propagation did not occur. An emitted REFUSE code is insufficient.
3. **Changed-condition / termination:** repeat the same permitted candidate after material state or witness conditions change; verify loss of effect authority and preserve the original evidence.
4. **Alternate physical path:** identify other routes through which the forbidden consequence could occur; observe them separately, without equating one stopped path with universal route closure.

For any released physical result, provide frozen configuration identifier, test conditions, measured effect or non-effect, uncertainty/calibration, sensor independence, changed-condition negative control, event/receipt digests, replay, and distinct witness identity. Publication requires safety and commercial clearance.

## Source and standing

The controlled Library source **KCD Unified Mechanism and Application Architecture v2.0** corrects an earlier reduction that treated an electromagnetic object-positioning rig as the entire KCD mechanism; **KCD-A1 Phase 0B** is an engineering formalization with open physical verification and release gates, **not an attached completed laboratory proof**. These sources are indexed here only by name and evidentiary status. No drawings, coil specifications, construction geometry, internal operators or physical blueprints are released by this page.

**Site standing:** formal physical examination categories and requirements only. **Instrumented physical results attached here: zero.**
