# Original physical-mechanism examination index — public-safe selection

**Purpose:** a narrow, public-facing path for critics, engineers, and reviewers to identify what physical mechanisms have been **formalized** and what would actually constitute an **observed physical proof**. It is not a release of controlled construction or a claim that the public website contains every original record.

**Custody status:** no private mathematics, URK operators, actuator designs, dimensions, winding tables, construction mappings, confidential calculations, pilot records, physical test data or proprietary diagrams are transferred by this index.

## Controlling original-source lineage

- **KCD Unified Mechanism and Application Architecture v2.0** — the corrected, controlling internal mechanism statement, Snyder / Revita; distinguishes the protected-interior/surrounding-relation design inversion from any one apparatus, and separately categorizes candidate field carriers.
- **KCD-A1 Phase 0B Complete Formalization v1.0** — a bounded electromagnetic apparatus engineering-control record, Snyder / Revita; explicitly leaves numerical freeze, energization, laboratory measurement, qualification, independent witnesses and other hardware release gates open.
- **KCD-A1 Preliminary Engineering Blueprint Package (P0, 14 July 2026)** — controlled candidate construction documentation for *one* possible low-energy electromagnetic test face, not the complete KCD architecture and not a certified fabrication release.

These originals are **identified, not reproduced or attached here**. The full private records require their own release approval. A document title is provenance, not proof of a completed physical test.

## Prioritized physical examination objects

### PH-01 — Protected interior and changing exterior relation

**Object:** the protected interior is held within declared invariants while a proposed change occurs in a controllable surrounding relation. It is the **surrounding relation**, not necessarily the payload, that is tested for admissible physical consequence.

**Publicly examinable duty:** declare interior invariants, the candidate exterior change, independent measurement points, precondition, effect observation, and failure/negative control. Differentiate protected-interior preservation from simply observing object displacement.

**Current source status:** formalized in the corrected KCD v2.0 record. **Instrumented physical result attached here: none.**

### PH-02 — Independent loss-of-standing termination in an electromagnetic witness

**Object:** one KCD-A1 candidate uses an independently gated, instrumented physical carrier to examine whether an attempted field effect can remain non-formed after a governing condition fails.

**Publicly examinable duty:** establish instrument independence, frozen configuration, non-bypass of the physical effect channel, no actuation after revocation, negative control, measured non-effect with uncertainty, and preserved run evidence.

**Current source status:** Phase 0B engineering controls formalized; independent hardware test and energized measurement are **not established by the material published here**. KCD-A1 is only **one electromagnetic embodiment**, never the whole KCD mechanism.

### PH-03 — Vibration and acoustic propagation paths

**Object:** bounded wave/propagation relations involving attenuation, reflection, isolation, transfer or rerouting around a protected interior. These are distinct application carriers, not assumed interchangeable.

**Publicly examinable duty:** fixed source and boundary, witness calibration, route-by-route propagation measurement, actual non-propagation or controlled reroute, independent negative control and repeated changed-condition result.

**Current source status:** mechanism/application classes formalized in KCD v2.0. **Executed instrumented physical case attached here: none.**

### PH-04 — Thermal burden routing and composite boundary

**Object:** bounded thermal transfer/dissipation and, where the carrier is genuinely composite, simultaneous preservation of every load-bearing domain's own obligations. One field's apparent success cannot silently stand in for another's.

**Publicly examinable duty:** record heat entering, leaving, and stored with uncertainty; measure the protected-zone limits; challenge altered boundary conditions; and maintain separate evidence for any additional domains.

**Current source status:** mechanism/application classes formalized in KCD v2.0. **Executed physical carrier attached here: none.**

## One technical challenge standard for all four

A physical claim reaches an observed-evidence tier only through a frozen apparatus and operating envelope, calibrated independent measurement, positive and negative controls, same-condition replay, changed-condition repetition, an accounted actual effect or measured non-effect, retained receipts, and explicit limitations. An independently reproduced tier additionally needs a genuinely separate qualified examination.

**A printed refusal, simulated actuator state, drawing, or signed narrative does not count as measured physical non-formation.** The physical outcome must be observed on the consequence-bearing path. A failed release gate remains failed; no run or witness is inferred from a completed design formalization.

## Non-transfer boundary

The physical mechanism family is not released as build instructions. Participants are not granted custody of the protected foundation, kernel, substrate, law-generating mathematics, actuator construction, numerical configuration, or historical original run packets.

A technical critic can challenge the **exact bounded published assertion** with a precise contradiction. Their inability to see protected construction does not license promotion of an unverified claim, but neither does missing public attachment invalidate existing records that are held separately.

**The Deagle Proof Estate is the current, selectively tailored public examination desk, not the complete historical science, engineering, mechanism, pilot, or commercial record.**
