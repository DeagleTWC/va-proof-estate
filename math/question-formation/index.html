Domain before answer.

A question must have mathematical standing before its answer can be called universally true.

Exact words: If ab = 0, must a = 0 or b = 0?

Those words alone do not say which number system or operations are intended.

Reading A · ordinary integers

Yes.

If two integers are nonzero, their absolute values are each at least 1. Their product therefore cannot be zero.

PROVEN · within ℤ

Reading B · integers modulo six

No.

[2] × [3] = [6] = [0]

Neither [2] nor [3] is the zero residue class.
