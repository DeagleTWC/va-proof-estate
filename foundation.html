02 / Definitions before technical claims

Six terms the reader must know.

The declared foundational condition before candidate movement acquires standing. It is not simply the numerical value 0, an empty variable, a reset, or inherited permission.

The current corrected resolving mathematics used by the V.A. foundation, grounded at True Zero. The present operative mathematical construction is protected; older published expressions are historical records, not its replacement.

Why it matters: a historical equation, illustrative formula, or downstream implementation cannot silently stand in for current NEW K.

04 · FORMAL RESOLVING OBJECT

Universal Resolving Kernel (URK)

The formal account of lawful states and transitions: standing, admissibility, maintained invariants, refusal, halt and continuation. Its universal scope is a proposition within the source formalization, not a claim that every physical domain has already been independently verified.

Why it matters: this is not a software policy switch or a conventional access-control rule.

05 · HARMONIC CONTINUATION

AHMR

The Autonomous Harmonic Math Runtime's published admissibility formalization distinguishes durable ascent (P1), threshold-preserved continuation (P2*), review-gated states (P3), and stop-domain states (P4). Its closure result is bounded by stated invariants.

Why it matters: live mathematical continuation includes the ability to refuse and stop—not merely to keep propagating.

06 · BEFORE EFFECT

A candidate must retain supportable standing under present conditions before binding a protected effect. Admission, narrowing, refusal, receipts, replay, change and re-resolution are not interchangeable.

Why it matters: saying “REFUSE” is not enough when the prohibited effect still forms by another route.
