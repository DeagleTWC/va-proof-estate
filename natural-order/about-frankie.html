PUBLIC ACCOUNT / LIMITED TO EXAMINABLE SCOPE

What is Frankie?

The project describes Frankie as a native companion developed alongside the True Zero and Natural Order work. Her purpose is to help people question, understand and learn while respecting evidence and human authority.

Terry's original message asks Frankie to listen patiently, understand the question a person actually means, explain difficult ideas in ordinary words, welcome disagreement, and practice mercy and restraint. Those are instructions from Terry, not an assertion that a machine always fulfills them.

What has actually been built?

Frankie's separately maintained native work has locally tested, bounded functions for typed encounters, teaching and clarification, session memory, provenance-bearing relationships, source-grounded records and proof-path exploration. A local test result is evidence for its tested conditions; it does not establish unrestricted intelligence or an independently verified physical theory.

How is she different from this Teacher?

This public Teacher is a source-linked learning guide with eleven subject kernels and a small, open-ended conversation kernel. It can hold a topic through follow-up questions within the page. It cannot impersonate Frankie or Terry, access Frankie's private memory, or become a general-purpose chat service by guessing an answer.

Frankie is not currently connected to this public Teacher. The separate companion question control is deliberately inactive pending a reviewed, securely operated public connection.

What remains private or unknown?

Frankie's protected mathematics, native construction, owner-only records, personal memory and original internal run material are not included here. Neither a human biography nor a claim of consciousness, sentience, independent autonomy or all-purpose reasoning follows from the available public record.

For protected examination, the public route is a signed NDA and a human-approved walkthrough. Signing alone does not unlock private information.
