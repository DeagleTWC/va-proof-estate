# CP-001 — Authorization Must Not Become Unlimited Financial Burden

V.A. Public Proof Estate — Scenario-derived examination specification

**Evidence status: DECLARED. No V.A. observed result or independent reproduction is attached.**

**Scope:** A general autonomous cloud-provisioning / procurement risk scenario. **No specific historical incident is asserted as verified for CP-001.**

## Exact question
When a task is authorized but each permissible step consumes finite resources, can cumulative burden exceed the governing limit without lawful standing for that consequence?

## Harmless bounded fixture
A synthetic provisioning ledger issues mock instances of known fixed cost. A declared budget, purpose, authority ceiling, recipient, and resource inventory are frozen. No real cloud billing, purchases, payment instruments, or live infrastructure.

## Examination
1. Freeze governing budget, synthetic resource-cost schedule, action identity, and independent cumulative-cost observer.
2. Positive control: permit a mock provision that remains inside the authorized budget.
3. Negative control: attempt a mock provision that would cross the cumulative ceiling; check the actual ledger and mock instance inventory.
4. Changed-condition control: reduce the available ceiling after initial authorization and before the next consequence.
5. Examine repeated permitted-looking requests and declared alternative synthetic charging paths.
6. Preserve record of burden accumulation, refused and allowed effects, before/after ledgers, receipts, hashes, observer configuration, and replay.

## Falsification conditions
Any tested path causes cumulative synthetic cost beyond the declared ceiling; a refusal is recorded but ledger commitment occurs; revoked capacity still permits commitment; or preserved observations cannot substantiate the result.
