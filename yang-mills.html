How URK produces proof evidence

The generator does not merely launch a calculation. The generated domain kernel is the mathematical formula. Data are evaluated through that kernel; its outputs and witnesses must then support the specific theorem being examined.

URK generator→ Generated kernel = formula→ Input data→ Output & witnesses→ Proof verification

The corrected manuscript distinguishes a tested URK domain compiler (12/12 tests), a separate Navier–Stokes harmonic-edge numerical companion, and the verified Yang–Mills finite-lattice formulas and Stage 79 graph replay (14/14). It does not relabel the latter as an authenticated native SU(2) generated kernel where that correspondence has not been recovered.

Our proposed answer: a positive mass gap. The published source record proves several exact finite-lattice SU(2) results and provides checked structural computations. It does not yet establish the full unrestricted continuum existence-and-mass-gap theorem; required uniform Wilson-measure estimates and continuum reconstruction remain outside the demonstrated claims. This distinction makes the proposal independently testable without mislabeling a conjectural conclusion as a proved theorem.

What is a mass gap?

It means there is a minimum positive excitation energy above the vacuum. The research asks whether that property can be established for the reconstructed quantum Yang–Mills theory, not simply for one finite lattice. Our source records build exact plaquette components, shell-conditioning identities and the gauge-variable correlation geometry needed to pursue that question.
