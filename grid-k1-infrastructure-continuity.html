Operative mathematical relationships

Continuity before consequence

A proposed continuation, degradation, or reroute must satisfy the entire coupled network's declared conditions before the modeled transition is admitted.

Reproduce the recorded mathematical run

Extract the evidence ZIP and run python reproducibility/verify_exact_output.py. It recomputes all seven exact rational examples without importing the producer, validates certificate and receipt hashes, and rejects a deliberately tampered witness. The original draft sources remain in author custody; the public evidence bundle preserves their hashes but not their full contents. The frozen synthetic run does not test cross-domain prerequisite attestation.

Evidence and limits

Proofs concern a finite generic conserved service-flow model with explicit rights, freshness, capacities, service minimums, monitored debt, and a declared prerequisite relation. They do not solve physical AC power flow, verify operator signatures, establish a deployed non-bypass controller, or show that physical cascading outages cannot occur. Novelty is not asserted for classical max-flow/min-cut mathematics.
